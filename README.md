# The World Today — Wednesday 25 November 2026

Entry for the make.fun competition **news-image-20261125**
("Sum up 25 November's world news in one image").

`main.py` copies the bundled `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`,
byte for byte, with no network access — two runs produce identical bytes.

## What the image shows

One editorial poster, five stories of Wednesday 25 November 2026:

1. **Centre — Strait of Hormuz:** an oil tanker ablaze in the narrow strait
   between rocky coasts. Iran's IRGC said two tankers struck naval mines and
   caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz; Yemen's Houthis also attacked Saudi
   Arabia's Ras Tanura refinery the same day.
2. **Top left — Kyiv:** an apartment block on fire with firefighters at work,
   after Russian strikes hit Kyiv (and Odesa) overnight.
3. **Top right — space:** a rocket and station hardware with an astronaut —
   China's Shenzhou-24 docked at the space station carrying a Pakistani
   national, the first foreign astronaut on a Chinese mission.
4. **Bottom left — the gavel and the chip:** a judge's gavel coming down on a
   smartphone/microchip — a US federal court found Huawei guilty of
   conspiracy to steal trade secrets and bank fraud.
5. **Bottom right — the split planet:** Earth half cracked and parched, half
   flooded — Pope Leo XIV voiced concern at the failure of the UN COP31
   climate talks to reach an agreement.
